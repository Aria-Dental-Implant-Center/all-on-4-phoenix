## Our Services

### All-on-4® Dental Implants
The All-on-4® treatment concept is a full-arch tooth replacement solution that uses just four strategically placed implants to support a complete set of fixed, non-removable teeth. This approach eliminates the need for bone grafting in most cases and allows patients to leave our office with a brand-new smile in a single day.

**Who is All-on-4 for?**
- Patients with most or all teeth missing
- Patients with severely failing or decayed teeth
- Patients currently wearing dentures who want a permanent, fixed alternative
- Patients who have been told they don't have enough bone for traditional implants

**What to expect:**
- Comprehensive 3D CT scan and consultation
- Customized treatment planning using digital workflow
- Placement of four implants per arch
- Same-day provisional teeth in most cases
- Final, permanent restoration delivered weeks later

---

### Single Dental Implants
For patients missing one or a few individual teeth, a single dental implant is the gold standard of tooth replacement. A titanium implant post is placed into the jawbone, an abutment is attached, and a custom porcelain crown is placed on top — creating a restoration that looks, feels, and functions just like a natural tooth.

**Benefits of single implants over bridges or partials:**
- Preserves surrounding bone and adjacent teeth
- No removal required — fixed permanently in place
- Matches the appearance of natural teeth
- Can last a lifetime with proper care

## Our Digital Workflow

Aria Dental Implant Center is at the forefront of digital implant dentistry. Our fully integrated digital workflow means greater precision, better outcomes, and a more predictable experience for every patient.

### 🖥️ 3D Cone Beam CT Scanning (CBCT)
Every implant patient begins with a high-resolution 3D CT scan of their jaw. This allows Dr. Mehranfar to evaluate bone volume, density, and anatomy in three dimensions — identifying the optimal implant position before a single incision is made.

### 📸 Intraoral Scanning
Instead of traditional impressions, Aria uses digital intraoral scanners to capture precise 3D models of your teeth and gums. This data feeds directly into our design software — eliminating the guesswork of analog impressions.

### 📐 Digital Treatment Planning
Using advanced software, Dr. Mehranfar virtually plans every implant placement before the procedure. The position, angle, and depth of each implant are mapped digitally — and in many cases, surgical guides are fabricated to ensure the plan is executed with pinpoint accuracy.

### 🦷 Photogrammetry
For full-arch cases, Aria uses photogrammetric scanning to capture the precise spatial relationship of implants after placement. This data is used to fabricate final restorations that fit with exceptional accuracy — reducing adjustment appointments and improving long-term outcomes.

### 🖨️ CAD/CAM In-House Milling & 3D Printing
Aria operates an in-house digital dental laboratory with CAD/CAM milling and 3D printing capabilities. This means faster turnaround, tighter quality control, and restorations that are designed, reviewed, and approved by Dr. Mehranfar before they ever reach the patient.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I know if I'm a candidate for dental implants?**
A: Most adults in good general health are candidates. Even patients with low bone volume may qualify for All-on-4 due to the angled implant placement technique. The only way to know for certain is a 3D CT scan and consultation with Dr. Mehranfar.

**Q: Is the procedure painful?**
A: Implant placement is performed under local anesthesia and sedation options are available. Most patients report that the discomfort is far less than they anticipated. Post-operative soreness is typically managed with over-the-counter pain medication.

**Q: How long does the process take?**
A: For All-on-4, most patients receive a full set of provisional teeth the same day as their implant surgery. The final, permanent restoration is typically delivered 3–6 months later once the implants have fully integrated with the bone.

**Q: Does Aria offer financing?**
A: Yes. Aria works with multiple financing partners to help patients afford their treatment. Our team will walk you through all available options during your consultation.
